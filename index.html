About

I am a PhD candidate in International and Advanced Japanese Studies at the University of Tsukuba. My research applies computational text analysis to Japanese online discourse, with a focus on toxicity, hate speech, and political communication.

Research interests: computational political communication; Japanese online discourse; online hostility, toxicity and hate speech; framing and agenda setting; AI discourse in Japan; text-as-data methods for Japanese.
